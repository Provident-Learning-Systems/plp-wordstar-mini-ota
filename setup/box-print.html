Box inserts for the 60 × 60 mm case, 12 to an A4 sheet: the top two rows are lid cards (60 mm, notched - a snug fit so the lid clicks), the bottom two rows are base cards (59 mm, so the paper doesn't bow). Print A4 portrait at 100% scale (no "fit to page"). Cut along the dashed lines; the short black ticks in the gutters line up with them. Sizes and corner radius are the --panel / --panel-back / --radius values at the top of this file; copies in the script at the bottom sets how many of each.
